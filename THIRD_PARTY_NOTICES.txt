-------------------------------------
Source: https://github.com/mrgudenheim/TacticsTemplateG/pull/3, timbermania,
commit 0ebc36fcbaac56dd30e02bf1b2707c71140bc2d0 (origin/add-sound-clean).

Toolchain/runtime notices
-------------------------
Windows SPU binaries use LLVM-MinGW 20250910 (UCRT, x86_64). Applicable LLVM
Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception and MinGW runtime notices are reproduced in
LICENSES/toolchain/. These standard compiler/OS runtimes are treated as System
Libraries, not as missing godot-cpp or application source. The exact toolchain
archive URL/hash is recorded in tools/audio/upstream.json. Unmodified general
purpose compilers, Python, SCons, and Godot are build/run prerequisites, not
bundled application source. If modifying or distributing those programs,
review their own source-delivery obligations separately.

Godot engine exports must include the engine's own full license/third-party
notices obtained from the actual export engine, in GODOT-NOTICES.txt beside the
executable. The release tool supplies these; they are not generated from a
possibly different developer machine's installed engine version.
