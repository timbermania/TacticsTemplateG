🔴 THE GPL-3.0 OBLIGATION IS GONE. It came from the two addons that were removed
and from the Effects selection's upstream root LICENSE, which has since been
relicensed; LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt and GPL-2.0.txt are no longer in this tree.
Paired corresponding-source delivery is therefore no longer REQUIRED.
tools/effects/package_release.py still produces a source archive beside the
binary, because a reproducible source snapshot is worth having on its own terms —
but that is now a choice, not a licence condition.

The ExMateria Sound and SPU addons, their native binaries and their PCSX-derived
SPU sources were REMOVED from this project. With them went third_party/
exmateria-sound, tools/audio, docs/audio-installation.md, the LLVM-MinGW toolchain
notices, the godot-cpp MIT notice and the GPL-2.0 text, none of which any remaining
code needs. No PCSX-Redux code remains: the only mentions of PCSX left in the tree
are comments citing the emulator as a behavioural reference.
